MEET FLOW

A canvas you can hear.

Flow is an ambient instrument and a living fluid canvas. Droplets add dye and momentum to an incompressible fluid simulation. When they land, their horizontal position selects a note from the current chord. Each color plays a different sampled instrument. Choose a palette swatch to keep new droplets in that one color and voice, including automatic rain and blooms. Enable Cycle colors beside the palette to restore variety.

THE FIELD GUIDE

Find your own way to play.

Sound holds voices and harmony; Layers holds the arpeggiator, percussion and atmospherics; Perform holds canvas tools, quantize and automatic droplets; Scene shapes colors and fluid; Mix brings together levels, effects and shared echo timing. Expand an editor when you need its finer controls. Record / Library and Community stay above the canvas.

01A rhythm beneath the color

Layers → Percussion adds four percussion voices and a sixteen-step beat studio. Start with a groove, edit hits and accents, or generate evenly spaced patterns and rotate them into new rhythms. Ten percussion sounds are synthesized locally with Web Audio, with swing, humanize, and independent levels. Percussion has its own reverb and delay amounts. Voices and drums share one echo-spacing setting, available in Mix and Beat studio. Both delay lines follow the effective percussion tempo, including half/double speed, with eighth-note, dotted-eighth, and quarter-note echoes. Their timed hits create visible ripples, add momentum to the existing fluid, or both. No new dye or melodic notes are added by percussion.

02A world behind the music

Layers → Atmospherics adds optional Soft rain, Tidal shore, Canopy wind, Summer night or Hearth. Seeded noise, gentle filters and tiny procedural grains synthesize each environment locally; no field recordings are used. Mix, Activity and Tone shape the layer, and whole-scene gestures can filter it or send it into space. Atmosphere settings and changes are included in saved and shared sessions, while audio stays local.

03Play with your whole hand

The Perform tab explains six gestures. Position shapes pitch, stereo placement, weight, tone, and length. Slow strokes make soft ribbons; quick strokes and flicks create short accents and jets. Circles make whirlpools and stereo motion. Hold still, then release a bloom of long chord tones. Vertical sweeps conduct a filter across the entire composition, which gently settles after release. Gesture intensity, a whole-scene toggle, and a reset let you choose the amount of expression.

UNDER THE SURFACE

A little science. A little atmosphere.

♫

The sound

Sound is powered by WebAudioFont by Sergey Surikov, with 28 instruments from the FluidR3 GM soundfont by Frank Wen. All instrument samples and the playback engine are served with this site. The sustained voices use gentle attack envelopes and extended releases for softer, overlapping swells. Eight experimental textures add crystalline rain, soundtrack and atmosphere tones, spectral brightness, alien choruses, echoes, and sci-fi signals, with individual slow filter sweeps. Reverb uses a locally generated stereo impulse response; delay uses a filtered feedback loop synchronized to the tempo.

The motion

The fluid uses semi-Lagrangian advection, pressure projection, and vorticity confinement.

Every droplet brings color, momentum, and a note into the same living canvas.
RECORD & CONNECT

Keep a moment. Share a little.

Your library & the community

Your live settings and recorded takes stay in this browser. The optional Community library lets you explicitly publish a selected take, listen to others and like favorites. Sign in with ChatGPT to publish or vote; browsing and listening are open to everyone.

Charts & your identity

Today charts use midnight UTC, This week covers the last seven days, and All time counts every active like. Public names and titles pass basic server-side text checks. Your account email is never displayed.

Sessions & exports

Shared sessions contain only notes, settings, gestures and visual data; audio uploads are not accepted. Flow synthesizes session loops during playback. Export loop WAV renders one cycle locally in real time. Earlier browser audio takes remain playable locally. Shared sessions can be up to 8 MB; removing your public track leaves your browser copy intact.
