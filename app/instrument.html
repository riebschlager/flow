Gesture guideSix ways to play · mouse, touch & keyboard
01

Touch & place

Left-click for a note. Height makes a clear change: bright and light at the top; warm, fuller and longer at the bottom. Left to right still chooses pitch.

02

Silk ↔ rush

Slow strokes make soft attacks, long notes and broad ribbons. Fast strokes make short, bright attacks and stronger fluid jets. Flick to release a crisp final spark.

03

Gather & bloom

Hold still to grow a ring, pull fluid inward and build reverb around the whole mix. Release to fan out four current-chord tones and a broad outward wave.

04

Orbit

Draw circles while painting, or select Orbit for direct control. Rotate the whole mix in stereo, bring out rhythmic echoes and stir a wide whirlpool. Release to let it settle.

05

Conduct · right-drag / Shift

Drag down to close the composition’s filter; up to open it. Move left or right to pan. Broad currents and a moving guide line follow your hand. This tool adds no droplets.

06

Space · Alt / Option

Drag up for more reverb and right for stronger tempo-synced echoes on voices, arpeggio and drums together. Wide rings and slow eddies reveal the space. Release to return smoothly.
