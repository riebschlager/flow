## Using your music

Export a clip or WAV from your Library to keep a copy on your device. Exports are made locally and are not uploaded. Before using a render in your own content, review the notices for Flow and its instrument sources.

Notices to link alongside the copy:
- Flow code: GPL-3.0-only (root LICENSE).
- WebAudioFont playback engine: GPL-3.0 (public/vendor/LICENSE-WebAudioFont.md).
- WebAudioFontData: bundled notice, MIT, copyright 2017 Srgy Surkv (public/vendor/LICENSE-WebAudioFontData.txt).
- FluidR3: MIT, with the bundled soundfont acknowledgements (public/vendor/LICENSE-FluidR3.txt).

The approved copy makes no claim about licensing or ownership of exported music.
